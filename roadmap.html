Sporafind Build Roadmap

From intelligence database to diaspora social platform. A phased build where each phase funds the next.

🌍 The Vision

Phase A (now): Read-only diaspora intelligence from Companies House data. Syndicates, recruiters, and researchers pay to search, filter, and export.
Phase B-D (future): Diaspora members claim their scraped profile, verify, and opt into paid connections. The platform becomes a two-sided marketplace: intelligence data on one side, social networking with tiered pricing on the other. Each phase is unlocked by revenue from the previous.

◆ Data Architecture

CURRENT

profiles (scraped)

Companies House officer records. Read-only, never modified by users. Has claimed_by FK to users as a nullable column — ready for Phase B.

PHASE B

user_profiles (new table)

Diaspora members who claim their scraped profile. Stores bio, photo, interests, verified status, and connection price tiers. Linked to profiles via FK.

PHASE C

pricing_tiers + connections (new tables)

pricing_tiers: per-user pricing for Chat, Intro, Meeting. connections: tracks who paid to connect, status, Stripe payment intents.

PHASE D

direct sign-ups

Diaspora members create profiles without a scraped record. Feeds, groups, events tables added. Full community platform.

◆ Strategic Decisions — zero-cost until revenue

Phase A buyer: Investment syndicates, recruiters, researchers
Phase B-C buyer: Same + diaspora members seeking visibility
Phase A pricing: Free + $19/mo paid + Enterprise (per-seat)
Phase C pricing: Platform takes 15% cut on connection fees
