Sporafind Fundraising Strategy

Pre-seed capital strategy — target $50-300K. Build prototype first, collect validation metrics, then ask for money. Includes the "quit my job" runway plan, validation milestones, and investor sequence.

◆ Fundraising Decisions

Target raise: $50-300K pre-seed
Target investor: Diaspora-focused funds + angel syndicates
Ask timing: Only after working prototype is live with real data
Warm up now: Send "building X, prototype in 4 weeks" intros — don't ask for money yet
Validation gate: Prototype + 5 beta users + engagement data before any pitch meeting
Target segment: Investment syndicates (not general VCs)
Quit trigger: $50K+ raise closes OR 5 beta users paying $19/mo OR ABLE 2026 acceptance
Founder salary: $3-6K/mo included in raise (not optional — this is your full-time commitment)
Validation metrics: Profile count, search/user, D7 return rate, beta-to-paid conversion, NPS
Use of funds: Founder salary (40-50%) + Bright Data $250 + OpenAI $50-100/mo + contractor + legal
Don't: Pitch for money before you have a live prototype. Don't quit before you have a plan.
Pitch angle: "Live prototype with real data, real users, real engagement. Help me go full-time."
