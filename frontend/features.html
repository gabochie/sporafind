Five-class segmentation engine

Every profile in Sporafind is automatically classified into one of five segment types using AI signal analysis across 14 data sources. This isn't keyword tagging — it's behavioural and positional inference.

  • Regular people — diaspora members, professionals, creatives, students
  • Leaders — politicians, diplomats, executives, academics with influence
  • Entrepreneurs — founders, operators, business owners (identified via company registrations, media, business filings)
  • Investors — angels, VCs, PE professionals, HNW/UHNW individuals
  • Organisations — associations, syndicates, professional bodies, cultural groups, think tanks
