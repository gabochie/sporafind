Why work with us

Built for impact.
Run on autonomy.

Four principles guide everything we do at Sporafind — from how we build product to how we treat each other.

Impact at Scale
Your work connects 4.2M+ Ghanaians globally to capital, opportunity, and each other. We measure success in outcomes, not outputs.
Build African Tech
We build from and for Africa. Our data, infrastructure, and talent pipeline centre Ghana and the continent — not Silicon Valley.
Data-Driven
Every decision starts with data. We experiment, measure, and iterate. Intuition is welcome. Evidence is required.
Global Team
We span 6 time zones, 8 countries, and a shared mission. Asynchronous by default, intentional about connection.
