FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I switch plans later?
Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately. Downgrades apply at the start of your next billing cycle. All data is preserved.
What does "Ghana scope" vs "Pan-African scope" mean?
Ghana scope limits search to Ghanaian diaspora profiles globally. Pan-African scope expands to Ghanaian + all 54 African nations' diaspora data — including Nigerians, Kenyans, South Africans, Ethiopians, and more — in a single search surface.
Is there a free trial?
Every plan comes with a 14-day free trial. No credit card required. Full access to all features in your chosen plan. Cancel anytime with one click.
How is AI confidence scored?
Each profile receives a confidence score (0–100%) based on how many data sources corroborate the profile, the freshness of the data, and the specificity of the match. Scores above 80% are considered high-signal and verified across 3+ sources.
Can I export data to my CRM?
Pro and Enterprise plans include native HubSpot and Salesforce integration. CSV and XLSX export is available on all plans. Notion and Airtable sync coming in Q3 2026.
What data sources power the intelligence?
Sporafind cross-references LinkedIn, Companies House, Crunchbase, public business registries, news media, academic databases, professional networks, NGO directories, diaspora association listings, and 8 other public and proprietary sources. We never access private data.
Is my data secure?
Yes. Sporafind is GDPR-compliant. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We never sell or share your search data or exports. Enterprise plans include dedicated data processing agreements and on-premise deployment options.
